03

Scoring system

Public federal data, built the way commercial flood and wind models are weighted

What goes into the score
  • Coastal floodingNRI coastal flood risk: storm surge and tidal flooding frequency times the value exposed, scored 0 to 100 nationally.
  • HurricaneNRI hurricane risk: modeled wind loss to buildings. Every Virginia ZIP carries some hurricane score; the coast carries the most.
  • Inland floodingNRI riverine and inland flood risk: the reason some Blue Ridge and Appalachian ZIPs rank High.
  • ResilienceNRI community resilience: local capacity to recover, used as a small adjustment.
  • AvailabilityCarrier models (flood zone determinations, wind pools) are property-level and not published by ZIP. This composite uses public federal data on the same ingredients.

Tier rules
  • ExtremeCoastal flood score 95+, or 50%+ of the ZIP's residents in tracts rated Relatively High or Very High for coastal flooding
  • HighCoastal flood score 85+, 20%+ of residents in high coastal-flood tracts, or inland flood score 95+
  • ModerateCoastal flood score 50+, inland flood score 85+, hurricane score 90+, or any single tract with a coastal flood score of 99+
  • LowEverything else
Reference points
  • Bay and island ZIPsChincoteague Island 23336 scores 83.7 (Extreme); Poquoson 23662 scores 77.8 (Extreme); Norfolk 23510 scores 75.0 (Extreme)
  • Virginia BeachVirginia Beach 23451 scores 46.8 (High); Virginia Beach 23454 scores 47.3 (Moderate); Virginia Beach 23456 scores 43.2 (Moderate). Large ZIPs blend waterfront streets with inland neighborhoods, so a Moderate ZIP can still hold flood-zone homes.
  • Inland metrosFairfax 22030 scores 25.6 (Low); Richmond 23220 scores 19.5 (Low); Roanoke 24012 scores 28.3 (Low)

07

Flood as a cross-sell

Homeowners policies exclude flood; the coastal ZIPs are where it sells

Why it matters here
  • Coverage gapStandard homeowners policies do not cover flood. In High and Extreme ZIPs a flood quote belongs in the first conversation, not the renewal.
  • Virginia BeachThe city is a Class 7 community in FEMA's Community Rating System, which gives residents a 15% discount on NFIP flood premiums.
  • HurricaneEvery Hampton Roads ZIP carries a hurricane score above the national median; wind and hurricane deductibles are worth explaining at quote time.
  • Inland22 High ZIPs are inland-flood driven (Rockingham, Nelson, Alleghany and the southwest), a smaller but real flood audience outside the coast.
